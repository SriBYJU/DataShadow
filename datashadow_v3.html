The Four Systems

🧠

AI Negotiation Engine (Claim 1)

Claude-powered agent scoring brand requests across four behavioral signals, computing dynamic prices, and executing transactions autonomously without per-request user input.

🛡️

Data Poisoning Shield (Claim 2)

Differential privacy noise injection applied per-transaction with a unique seed — brands receive valid signals but can never reconstruct a complete profile.

🔐

Consent Firewall by Domain (Claim 3)

Six life domains each with independent Open/Selective/Locked rules, pricing floors, and risk ratings encoding HIPAA and FCRA-adjacent restrictions.

📈

Financial Portfolio Engine (Claim 4)

Asset valuation, time-decay depreciation, yield calculation, and diversification scoring — personal data treated as a managed financial portfolio.

💡

Adaptive Intelligence Layer (Claim 5)

Claude reads your transaction history, identifies yield patterns and depreciation anomalies, and proactively surfaces portfolio optimization recommendations — learning from your decisions over time.
