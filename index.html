Why this matters — cybersecurity context

Differential privacy — the academic concept behind this shield — is used by Apple and Google to collect aggregate data without exposing individuals. DataShadow applies this same principle to personal data transactions: brands get statistically valid behavioral signals, but can never reconstruct a complete surveillance profile of any individual user. The noise injection algorithm varies per transaction, meaning even the same brand buying your data twice receives two different datasets — preventing correlation attacks across purchases.

About DataShadow

🧠

AI Negotiation Engine

A Claude-powered agent that scores incoming brand data requests using four behavioral signals — recency, exclusivity, demand match, and profile rarity — then autonomously prices and executes transactions without per-request user input.

🛡️

Data Poisoning Shield

Before any approved data leaves the system, a differential privacy algorithm injects calibrated noise into non-essential fields. Brands receive statistically valid signals — but can never reconstruct a complete surveillance profile. Based on real academic research from MIT and Stanford.

🔐

Consent Firewall by Life Domain

Data is segmented into life domains — athletics, entertainment, finance, health, social, location — each with independent consent rules, pricing floors, and risk ratings that mirror real privacy law distinctions like HIPAA and FCRA.

📈

Financial Portfolio Layer

Personal data is treated as a financial asset with live market valuation, yield calculation, depreciation curves, diversification scoring, and risk ratings — applying portfolio management principles to data ownership for the first time.
