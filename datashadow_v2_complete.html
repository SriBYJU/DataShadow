About DataShadow

🧠

AI Negotiation Engine

A Claude-powered agent that scores incoming brand data requests using four behavioral signals — recency, exclusivity, demand match, and profile rarity — then autonomously prices and executes transactions without per-request user input. Grounded in a $352B open data market identified in PwC's 2025 Value in Motion report.

🛡️

Data Poisoning Shield

Before any approved data leaves the system, a differential privacy algorithm injects calibrated noise into non-essential fields. Brands receive statistically valid signals — but can never reconstruct a complete surveillance profile. Based on real academic research from MIT and Stanford; each transaction produces a unique noised dataset to prevent correlation attacks.

🔐

Consent Firewall by Life Domain

Data is segmented into life domains — athletics, entertainment, finance, health, social, location — each with independent consent rules, pricing floors, and risk ratings that mirror real privacy law distinctions like HIPAA and FCRA. Domain-locked data is never sold, regardless of offer price.

📈

Financial Portfolio Layer

Personal data is treated as a financial asset with live market valuation, yield calculation, time-decay depreciation curves, diversification scoring, and risk ratings — applying portfolio management principles to data ownership for the first time.

Patent status

Provisional patent pending · USPTO

A computer-implemented method for autonomous real-time pricing of personal data access requests, comprising: (1) classifying requests against behavioral signal profiles; (2) computing dynamic access prices using recency, exclusivity, demand, and rarity signals; (3) applying differential privacy noise injection before fulfillment; and (4) executing grant/denial decisions autonomously without per-transaction user input, logged in a tamper-evident consent ledger. The combination of autonomous negotiation, domain-segmented consent architecture, and differential privacy is novel and has no prior art in consumer data marketplaces.
